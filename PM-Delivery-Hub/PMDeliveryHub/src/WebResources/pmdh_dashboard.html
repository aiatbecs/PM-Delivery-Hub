People and project membership

Directory identities are assigned explicitly, never inferred from transcript names. Linked human users gain access through the project's owner team. External people without an app account can own work but cannot sign in.

Designated managers are assigned the PMDH Management security role by an authorized environment administrator. Review existing roles before sharing: Dataverse grants are additive; broader roles can confer additional access.
