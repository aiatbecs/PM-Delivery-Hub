Meeting Review

Development workflow. Use synthetic data until project access is tested. No paid AI is called. Copilot output is untrusted: review every proposal and its evidence. Email is never sent here.

1. Process input in your approved Copilot account

Supply the transcript and screenshots directly to Microsoft 365 Copilot. This page does not process images. Retain the originals in your approved storage. Copilot should quote source evidence with timestamps or screenshot labels.

2. Import proposals (not official records)

Maximum 50 proposals per import. Keep the same meeting sourceKey and candidate keys on retries. Keys accept letters, digits, hyphens and underscores only; no colons. A changed candidate with an existing key is an error; it is not silently overwritten.

3. Review proposals

Approve applies the displayed edited content in one transaction. Reject leaves official records unchanged. Reviewed proposals are immutable; submit a new candidate for further changes.

Approved meeting records

Generic drafts, not your corporate templates. Nothing is uploaded to SharePoint or emailed automatically.
